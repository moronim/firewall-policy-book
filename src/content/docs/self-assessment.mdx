Score each statement from 0 to 5, where 0 means no and 5 means yes, fully implemented. Your total places you on the four-stage maturity model from Chapter 6, and the section scores show which area holds you back.

:::tip[Get a PDF report of your result]
Once every statement is scored, you can **save a PDF report** to share with your team or sponsor, or **copy a short summary** into an email or ticket. The buttons are in the result panel, and they switch on when the last statement is scored.

Your answers stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere; the report is produced by your browser's print dialog.
:::

## Reading your score

The stage bands are editorial decision rules for this self-assessment, not industry benchmarks, so treat them as a directional heuristic. Maturity is capability-specific, and the section scores can reveal a mixed profile, strong governance with weak automation, say, that the total alone hides. A score near a boundary means you are transitioning, which matters more than the exact number.

Chapter 15 of the book turns the result into quick wins and an 18- to 24-month roadmap, and the [checklists](../checklists/) and [templates](../templates/) on this site support the first steps.
